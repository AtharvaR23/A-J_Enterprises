ABOUT A&J

A focused steel & PEB team, growing fast.

A&J Enterprises delivers turnkey structural steel and pre-engineered building (PEB) works — design, detailing, fabrication, supply and erection — for industrial, warehousing and infrastructure clients.

We run our own fabrication yard and erection crews, which means fewer handoffs, tighter tolerances, and a single point of accountability from the first structural drawing to the last torque-checked bolt.

  • In-house structural design & detailing team
  • Own fabrication shop with CNC cutting & welding bays
  • Trained erection crews with rigging & safety certification
  • Quality control against IS 800 & MBMA standards

02

How a project moves through our yard.

A fixed five-stage sequence keeps every structure — from a single shed to a multi-bay plant — on the same disciplined path.

1

Survey & design

Site survey, load assessment and structural design against applicable codes.

2

Detailing & approval

Fabrication drawings, BOQ and client sign-off before a single cut is made.

3

Fabrication

Cutting, drilling, welding and shop-painting in our own fabrication bays.

4

Transport & erection

Sequenced delivery and crane erection by our trained rigging crews.

5

QC & handover

Torque checks, alignment survey and documented handover pack.
